Batman (Bruce Wayne)

Batman is a fictional superhero from DC Comics, created by artist Bob Kane and writer Bill Finger. He first appeared in Detective Comics #27 in 1939 and has become one of the most recognised characters in the world.

By day he is Bruce Wayne, a billionaire from Gotham City. As a child he saw his parents, Thomas and Martha Wayne, murdered. He turned that loss into a promise to protect others, training his mind and body to fight crime.

Batman has no superpowers. His strength comes from discipline, intelligence, detective skills and determination, which is why so many people find him
